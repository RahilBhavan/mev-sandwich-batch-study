# Font provenance

IBM Plex Sans Regular and SemiBold downloaded October 11, 2026 from the [IBM Plex repository](https://github.com/IBM/plex), paths `packages/plex-sans/fonts/complete/ttf/IBMPlexSans-Regular.ttf` and `IBMPlexSans-SemiBold.ttf`. `LICENSE.txt` was fetched from that repository root and is retained verbatim. The renderer embeds the license in standalone HTML and includes it in each export directory.

This font asset is independently licensed upstream; no Glitch Cat Club stylesheet, renderer or font package was copied.

Anton Regular and its `OFL.txt` downloaded October 11, 2026 from the Google Fonts repository, `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/anton/Anton-Regular.ttf` and adjacent `OFL.txt`. Font saved as `Anton-Regular.ttf`; license saved verbatim as `Anton-LICENSE.txt`. Both licenses are embedded and exported. No imagery or assets from Nick Ho’s site are redistributed.
